# WeatherFlow

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People checking local conditions and planning the next hours or days on a phone or desktop. This is a public portfolio application.

## Product Purpose

Find a city or use device location, understand current conditions, and compare the hourly and seven-day outlook.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve city search, geolocation, current weather, seven-day forecast, weather details, light/dark themes, metric/imperial units, recent cities, last-city restoration, keyboard access, and cancellation of stale requests. Improve these comprehensively, including hourly forecasts. Keep the incumbent dependency-free HTML/CSS/JavaScript stack and GitHub Pages compatibility. Real weather comes from Open-Meteo; unavailable data must never become invented live data.

## Brand Commitments

WeatherFlow name. English UI confirmed by the user, who delegated design and complete implementation. User's related projects Forge and NOVA are anti-references for a repeated interface.

## Evidence on Hand

Original index.html, style.css, script.js, README.md and incumbent screenshots in screenshots/. No testimonials, business metrics, or commercial claims.

## Product Principles

- Data freshness and location remain explicit.
- A forecast is a prediction, not an observation or safety alert.
- Missing, offline, loading, and error states are actionable and honest.
- The main city search works with keyboard and touch.
